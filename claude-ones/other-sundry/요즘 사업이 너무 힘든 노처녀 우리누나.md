## 1. Introduction and Background

Joe Tube introduces his goal to help his older sister, Yuri, whose business has been struggling recently . After reading social media posts about luxury hotels in Korea renowned as "business hotspots" (famous locations believed to bring good fortune to businesses), he decides to visit them with her .

Before leaving, the group shares a meal together at home .

- Joe Tube and Jae-yeok discuss daily life and home interactions .
- They discuss Yuri's upcoming business-blessing hotel tour .
- Jae-yeok jokingly mentions wanting to pray for clean-up contents and meeting pretty women .

> 💡 **Purpose of the Tour**
>
> The primary goal of the day's trip is to visit high-energy "business hotspot" hotels across Seoul to receive positive fortune for Yuri's struggling business .

## 2. Visiting the First Hotel: Grand Hyatt Seoul

The group travels to Grand Hyatt Seoul, located in a mountain setting . 

- They discuss Saju (four pillars of destiny) elements, noting Yuri's lack of "fire" and "wood" energy, which guides their choice of location .
- Upon arrival, Yuri touches various stone fixtures and decorations in the lobby to absorb positive energy for her business .
- They admire the hotel's atmosphere and interior design .
- They order drinks in the lobby lounge, noticing high prices such as 18,000 KRW for a single glass of juice .
- They quickly drink their beverages to move on to the next location .

## 3. Visiting the Second Hotel: The Shilla Hotel

The group proceeds to The Shilla Hotel to continue their energy-gathering tour .

- Upon arrival, they observe a massive crowd waiting in the lobby .
- They check the waiting list for the hotel's famous mango bingsu (shaved ice) and find a queue of 52 teams .
- Due to the long wait, they decide to skip eating bingsu at Shilla Hotel .
- They walk around the hotel grounds and admire the Yeongbin-gwan section, soaking in the venue's reputation for weddings and big events .

> 💡 **Grand Hyatt Seoul**
>
> - Quiet, mountain-side setting 
> - Stone decorations touched for good fortune 
> - High beverage prices in the lounge
> 💡 **The Shilla Hotel**
>
> - High-density crowd in the lobby 
> - 52-team waiting list for mango bingsu 
> - Famous Yeongbin-gwan venue atmosphere

## 4. Visiting the Third Hotel: Four Seasons Hotel Seoul

The group heads to Gwanghwamun to visit the Four Seasons Hotel .

- On the way, they highlight the energy of the Gwanghwamun area and its historical landmarks .
- At the Four Seasons Hotel lounge, they order a high-end cake dessert costing 90,000 KRW .
- To save money, they decline ordering extra drinks and request water instead .
- Jae-yeok enjoys the cake dessert enthusiastically, eating most of it while the group jokes that he is absorbing all the business energy intended for Yuri .

## 5. Walking Through Gwanghwamun Square and Final Evening

After leaving the Four Seasons Hotel, the group explores Gwanghwamun Square and nearby historic sites .

- They view the Statue of Admiral Yi Sun-sin and the Statue of King Sejong the Great .
- Yuri mentions visiting Gwanghwamun Square for the first time in many years .
- They walk toward Gyeongbokgung Palace, capturing positive patriotic and historical energy .
- They finish their outing at a local tavern with drinks and food, celebrating the end of the hotel energy tour and wishing Yuri business success .

> "To help Yuri's business succeed, we explored 5-star hotels across Seoul today."
