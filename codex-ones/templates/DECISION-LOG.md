## Status rules

- **Proposed:** not authorized to shape implementation yet.
- **Accepted:** owner and effective date are recorded.
- **Rejected:** reason is retained so the question is not reopened without new evidence.
- **Superseded:** replacement decision ID is recorded; history is not deleted.
